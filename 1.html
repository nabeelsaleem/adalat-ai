Reality check (physics): The diagram claims 5 kW out for 1 kW of pumping. Energy from falling water is m·g·h: 140 kg × 9.81 m/s² × ~4.5 m ≈ 6.2 kJ per full load of buckets. Lifting that same 140 kg back to the top tank needs at least the same 6.2 kJ — and real pumps, chains, gears, generators and batteries each lose 10–40 %. So the pump always consumes more than the generator produces; the machine would slow and stop within seconds unless an outside energy source (grid, solar, fuel) runs the pump. The animation below is driven by a fixed motor speed to show the mechanism, not a self‑sustaining energy loop.
